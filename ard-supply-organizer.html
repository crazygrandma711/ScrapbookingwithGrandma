✂️ Card Size Guide

This guide helps you choose the right card size, cut your cardstock correctly, and match envelopes with ease.

⭐ Standard Card Sizes

  • A2 — 4.25" × 5.5" — Most common size; fits standard A2 envelopes.
  • A6 — 4.5" × 6.25" — Slightly larger; great for photo cards.
  • A7 — 5" × 7" — Elegant and formal; perfect for detailed designs.
  • Slimline — 3.5" × 8.5" — Tall, trendy, great for scene cards.
  • Mini Slimline — 3" × 6" — Cute, modern, perfect for quick cards.
  • Square Cards — 5" × 5" or 6" × 6" — Bold layouts; requires extra postage.

⭐ Specialty Card Styles

  • Gatefold — Opens from both sides; fancy and interactive.
  • Z‑Fold — Fun, layered, great for scenes.
  • Accordion — Multiple panels; perfect for birthdays and holidays.
  • Shaker Cards — Clear window with sequins; adds dimension and fun.
