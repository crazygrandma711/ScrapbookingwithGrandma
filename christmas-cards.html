How to Make Beautiful Christmas Cards

🎨 Choose a Style

Pick a theme such as traditional, modern, religious, or whimsical to guide your colors and embellishments.

🖼️ Build Your Layout

Start with a background layer, add borders, place your focal image, and finish with embellishments.

✨ Add Embellishments

Use stars, bows, trees, snowflakes, or tags to add dimension and personality to your card.

✂️ Use Punch-Outs

Punch-outs make it easy to add shapes without cutting by hand. Layer them for extra depth.

💌 Add a Sentiment

Use stickers, stamps, or printed labels to add greetings like “Merry Christmas” or “Joy to the World.”
