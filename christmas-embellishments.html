How to Use These Embellishments

✨ Layering

Start with a background color, then add embellishments in clusters. Layer stars, trees, bows, or tags to create depth.

🎨 Color Matching

Use the color palette for each theme to match cardstock, stickers, and borders. This keeps your layout cohesive.

📌 Clustering

Group embellishments in corners or around photos. Clusters make your pages feel intentional and polished.

🖼️ Borders

Create borders using ribbons, snowflakes, candy canes, or sparkle trails. Borders frame your photos beautifully.

💡 Add Texture

Use felt, ribbon, glitter glue, or metallic pens to add dimension and texture to your layouts.
